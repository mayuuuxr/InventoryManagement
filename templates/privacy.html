Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}. Plain words, no legal fog.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter stays on this shop PC, in a single database file. Nothing is uploaded to any cloud, and there is no account on any outside server.

What is stored

Usernames and PINs (stored hashed, never as plain text), materials, inward and outward entries, and an activity log of who changed what. That is all.

What is never collected

No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no location, no contacts. The app makes no network calls at all except an optional 2-second update check, and only when the owner opens it.

On this device

Your browser keeps a sign-in cookie (8 hours), a security cookie, and dismissal ticks for alerts. Clearing browser data signs you out. Nothing leaves the machine.

Backups

The app copies the database to a Backup folder on every start. Copy that folder to a pen drive monthly. Whoever holds the pen drive holds the data, so keep it safe.

Your responsibility

Keep PINs private, lock the PC when away, and delete staff logins when staff leave. Questions: ask your shop owner.
