Terms and Conditions

Last updated: October 15, 2026. Plain words, no legal fog.

What this app is

nibba is the shop's own stock register on its own PC: inward entries, outward entries, live balances, low-stock alerts, and reports. One payment, no subscription, no internet needed to run.

Who may use it

Only logins the shop owner creates. The owner is an administrator; staff may add entries and view stock. Staff may edit or delete only their own entries. Sharing PINs is not allowed.

Correct entries matter

Balances are calculated from what is entered. Check date, quantity, and material before saving. Wrong entries should be corrected the same day; the activity log records every change.

Backups are the owner's job

The app keeps automatic daily copies in the Backup folder, but a dead disk without a pen-drive copy still means data loss. Copy Backup to a pen drive every month.

Updates

Updates arrive through the in-app update page and never touch saved entries. A verified backup is taken before every update; if a backup fails, the update refuses to run.

Provided as is

The app is provided as is for running the shop's stock. Support covers installation, training agreed at purchase, and fixing defects. New features are quoted separately.
