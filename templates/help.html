Help

Signed in as {{ user.username }} ({% if user.is_superuser %}owner{% else %}{{ user.role }}{% endif %}).

Daily entries

  • Open Purchase to record stock bought, Sale to record stock sold.
  • Pick the material, enter date and quantity, save. A sale deducts automatically, oldest purchase first.
  • Balance updates by itself. You can't sell more than it shows.

Sign-in and PIN

  • Username + 4-digit PIN from your admin.
  • 5 wrong tries locks you for 1 hour.
  • Forgot PIN? Only your admin can reset it.

This PC & privacy

  • Everything runs on this PC only — no internet needed, no other device connects.
  • Saving needs the shop server running (the hidden window started by the nibba Shop icon).
  • Cookies and this-PC data: Sign-in cookie (8 hours), security cookie. No ads, no tracking, nothing shared.
{% if user.role == 'manager' %}

Your access

  • Managers have full access like the owner: entries, materials, staff logins, reports.
  • Everyone sees all entries and true stock balances; only edit/delete stay own-only.
  • Activity log (who did what) is owner-only. You won't see it.
{% else %}

Your access

  • You see all entries and true stock balances across the shop.
  • Edit or delete your own entries only; for someone else's, ask your admin.
  • Setup pages are owner-only.
{% endif %}

Something not working?

  • "Only … available" means stock just ran low. Enter a smaller qty.
  • Same date must have different times for repeat entries.
  • Anything else: tell your admin exactly what you tapped.
